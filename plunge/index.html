Why it exists

Those apps sell every SKU. This one runs your visit.

Pool Math is a calculator. Pool Nerd is a store. The Plunge is the visit: test kit, cups, pump hours, time-of-use, and a map of the pad you actually stand at.

Five tabs

Everything in one sheet

Opens on Chem. Pad photo sits next to the file.

01 Chem

This visit, then Save

Type this kit's FC, pH, TA. Want stays. Now does not persist. Last test is a hint, not a fake reading. CSI waits on pH, TA, CH, and water temp. Soda or acid, not both. Chlorine last. Pump on.

02 Clock

RPM cubed, hour by hour

Rest, swim, spa, vac. Skim so weirs tick. Heat in the cheap window. Forced off at peak. Salt cell only rides hours with real flow.

03 Log

Tests, charts, and the shelf

Every Save lands here: FC, pH, TA, pours. Charts so you can see if you are drifting, not just the last visit. Last / min / max on the same picture. Shelf and days-left so you know when to buy jugs. JSON download is the backup.

04 Pad

Your pad, labeled

Photo of the equipment. What each valve and unit is for. Daily vs spa vs vac as presets. Manual lines vs the ones the app should turn.

05 Power

Heat, gallons, your utility

Heat type then model. Search a decade of heat pumps, gas, and pumps. Water from L/W/D. Attached spa is extra volume on one body. Pick a utility plan. Chart is house+pool kW with peak in pink.

First weeks after plaster: liquid chlorine, rest before the cell, robot later. The sheet can watch that clock. It is not a sixth tab.

Personal

Not locked to 6500 forever

Name the pool. Surface. Sanitizer. Length, width, shallow, deep. Attached spa adds gallons to one body. Override dose gal if you measured. Utility and plan, or custom cents in Fine print.

Class rates, not your bill. Cups from your jugs, not Amazon SKUs.

How it runs

Local first. No login.

Everything lives in the browser. No automation-app password. No account. Open the sheet. Mobile web is first: 44px taps, 16px type so the phone does not zoom. Native iOS and Android later. Mild themes on the sheet: sand, night, lagoon.
