About

I'm a product leader with 15+ years in marketplace and transaction systems - real estate, auctions, and large-scale consumer platforms.

That startup-to-scale arc is the through-line. Time at Disney and other marketplaces reinforced the same lesson from a different angle - the process always breaks when it meets real people.

I still work closely with the people who run the workflows - not as a field operator, but as the person automating the repetitive parts. Third-party products fail when you design from assumptions; I'd rather learn what operators actually do than debate it in a conference room.

How I work

The customer isn't just the login.
Brokers, lenders, internal ops, and front-line operators all have to succeed for the product to stick. I design for the full chain, not a single persona slide.
Talk to users before you scale.
Especially for third-party tools: shadow the workflow, validate which fields move decisions, ship where pull rate proves adoption - not where a roadmap deck says you should.
Meet people in their tools.
Excel, Word, LOS, cloud folders - not a new portal nobody asked for. Context should show up where work already happens.
