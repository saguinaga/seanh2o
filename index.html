About

I'm a product leader with 15+ years in marketplaces and transaction systems. Real estate and auctions have been the through-line, but the problems repeat: multi-sided platforms, messy operations, configuration and data that has to hold up across states and teams.

I also spent time at Disney. Partner data rarely arrives in one clean format, even at scale. We normalized licensee feeds into a warehouse so analytics could be trusted. Good master data work starts with realistic ingestion, not expecting everyone on the same standard from day one.

Most of my Auction.com work centered on the core internal platform that powers the marketplace: catalog rules, transaction configuration, and the ops workflows wrapped around them. I spent a lot of time with operators and internal teams on what actually moves a decision. Salesforce was part of that picture too (custom build upstream and downstream of core), but the through-line for me was making the marketplace system trustworthy at scale.

How I work

The customer isn't just the login.
Brokers, lenders, internal ops, and front-line operators all have to succeed for the product to stick. I design for the full chain, not a single persona slide.
Talk to users before you scale.
Especially for third-party tools: shadow the workflow, validate which fields move decisions, ship where pull rate proves adoption - not where a roadmap deck says you should.
Meet people in their tools.
Excel, Word, LOS, cloud folders - not a new portal nobody asked for. Context should show up where work already happens.
