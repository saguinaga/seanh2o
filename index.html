About

I’m a product leader with 15+ years building marketplace and transaction systems. For me, the customer is anyone involved in a transaction — internal teams and external parties, buyers and sellers, the people on the ground and the customers they serve.

At Auction.com I was instrumental in evolving the backend platforms and tools that power the experience. I worked shoulder-to-shoulder with ground teams because I know the product only succeeds when every participant in the chain has what they need.

From years across marketplaces (including time at Disney), I’ve learned that no one adheres to a “standard” process. Buyers, sellers, and teams will give you what they have, when they have it, in whatever format makes sense to them. It’s our job to meet them in the middle — and give everyone the context to actually help.
