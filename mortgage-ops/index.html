    What I bring to specialty mortgage application analytics

    Unit of work: the loan application from intake through fund. Salesforce as the system of work for stages, reports, and where apps stall. Specialty paths in the demo include bridge, fix-and-flip, DSCR rental, term rental / portfolio, build-for-rent, and non-QM / alt docs. Approach and prototype only. Not a claim of employment at any named firm.

    Loan applications Salesforce in the real world Keep the book running

    About 15 years at Auction.com on loan-centric, multi-party systems. I am not a career LOS originations PM. What I bring is how I show up when the problem is messy: sit with the business, name where work actually stalls, dig when a summary is not enough, and ship the next clear change while production stays up.

    How I approach problems as they show up

    I do not start from a fixed roadmap template. I start from the pain in front of the room: a leadership number nobody trusts, a broker complaining about silence, a stage that means three different things, a spreadsheet that is still the real system of work.

    Pattern I use: listen (who needs what decision), define (what the stage or metric means in writing), measure what is true today (reports first; dig when the grain is missing), put people on the stuck work (queue with owners), then backlog the next fix so the dashboard is not theater.

    Build while the book is live

    At Auction.com we tried big platform rewrites that did not land. With Albert Low we got the core system that the company actually ran on through a real lift. The business did not pause for a clean room rebuild.

    That is the same shape as analytics while applications are still funding: improve the view and the process without pretending you can stop the car. I am comfortable shipping in that environment.

    Salesforce the way I actually used it

    Custom surfaces, intake, source of truth across feeds, list views that people open. I am not positioning as a Salesforce Administrator. I partner with whoever owns config and engineering.

    Standard reports and dashboards cover a lot. When I need the row under a summary (stale filter, definition drift, integration edge), I dig with SOQL / Inspector and field history, then tighten the definition or change the view if the business needs something different. Digging is not a claim that every report is wrong.

    Loan-centric, multi-party work

    Auction’s world was loans and cases and servicers and multi-state process, not a simple cart checkout. I will learn a specialty lender’s product box with SMEs. I do not claim day-one ownership of every guideline. I do claim comfort with multi-party handoffs, accuracy pressure, and systems that have to stay honest under real scrutiny.

    Eligibility and policy checks belong to the company as a quiet rules layer so applications can move. Brokers and borrowers should feel progress to a real yes, not a maze.

    AI as speed, not authority

    I use AI to go faster on drafts, queries, and prioritization ideas. Humans claim, clear, and own the call. In the prototype exception queue, AI ranks stuck applications; people act.
