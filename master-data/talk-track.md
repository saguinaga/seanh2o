## Questions to ask them

Pick **3 to 4** for a screening. Mix culture with role and current state. Listen more than you talk.

### Culture (you were recommended to ask)

Anchor terms if useful: **Tenets of Success** (ownership, integrity, practical forward motion), **Together Ingram Micro** (inclusion, collaboration across regions).

1. **PM + ops partnership**
   > "For vendor and product MDM, how do PMs typically partner with ops and data engineering? Who owns the workflow day to day, and where does product set direction vs facilitate?"

2. **Decisions at global scale**
   > "With teams across regions and BUs, how do you decide what ships first on master data: one BU as proof, or a global mandate from the start?"

3. **Year-one success**
   > "If someone is successful in this role in the first year, what would you point to: team dynamics, delivery rhythm, or specific outcomes leadership cares about?"

4. **Tenets in practice** (if conversation opens up)
   > "I saw you talk about Tenets of Success. What does that look like on a data platform team when architecture, ops, and product disagree?"

5. **Cross-functional trust** (if conversation opens up)
   > "Where do cross-functional relationships tend to be strongest on data work here, and where do they usually need the most intentional PM time?"

### Role and team

6. **Scope**
   > "How is the master data product org structured today? Is this role focused on vendor and product domains, or broader entity types as well?"

7. **Stakeholders**
   > "Who are the core partners I'd work with week to week: enterprise architecture, data engineering, business ops, commerce/Xvantage?"

8. **Why open**
   > "What prompted opening this role now?"

### Current state and pain

9. **Starting point**
   > "Where is vendor and product MDM today: greenfield, cleanup after acquisitions, or maturing platform with adoption gaps?"

10. **Top pain**
    > "What is the single biggest pain point leadership wants this role to address in the next 12 months?"

11. **Systems landscape**
    > "Which systems are the priority consumers of golden vendor and product records today: ERP, commerce, CRM, something else?"

### Outcomes and prioritization

12. **Metrics they care about**
    > "What metrics or outcomes does leadership already track for master data, and which ones are still missing?"

13. **Post-IPO pressure** (use lightly)
    > "Has being public again changed how platform and data investments get prioritized or measured?"

14. **Xvantage tie-in**
    > "How does the master data roadmap connect to Xvantage and AI-led selling from your perspective?"

### Close the screening

15. **Process**
    > "What are the next steps in the process, and who would I meet in the next round?"

16. **30-second version** if they say "any questions?" with no time left
    > "I'd love to hear how the master data team works with ops and engineering day to day, what the biggest near-term pain is, and what you'd expect someone in this role to prove in the first year."

### Avoid

- Generic "what's the culture like?" (too broad)
- Glassdoor-style negativity fishing
- Questions you could answer from the careers page without listening
- More than 4 questions in a 30-minute screen (quality over quantity)
