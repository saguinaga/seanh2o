Property & Financing

DSCR qualifies on rent vs PITIA. Rate is usually higher. 20-25% down is typical.

DSCR (debt service coverage) loans underwrite the property. Qualifying ratio is monthly rent divided by PITIA (principal and interest, taxes, insurance, HOA). Vacancy and management are your problem as the investor, not the lender's test. Typical down payment is 20-25%. Rate is usually higher than conventional.
